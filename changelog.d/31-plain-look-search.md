- The app drops its instrument look for everyday use: one readable sans font,
  no text under 12px, no tracking, glows or forced capitals, and labels in
  sentence case. Panels have plain names (Map layers, Cameras, Street view,
  Nearby, Places, Map look), the globe buttons say what they do, and every
  page shows Home · Shipments · Map tabs. One search box on the map finds
  containers, anything a layer lists, the layers themselves and places.
  Containers on the same ship now share one position on the globe, and boxes
  tracked before the loading date existed are re-tracked by themselves (#31).
