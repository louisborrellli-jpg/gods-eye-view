- A fresh boot is quiet by default. The **scope** — the circular viewport mask
  — starts off, so the map fills the window instead of being cropped to a
  circle with roughly a third of the viewport masked out; `#scope-toggle` in
  Display still turns it on, and a share link carrying it still restores it.
  The **Intel HUD** also starts off: its corner readouts are telemetry for
  someone who came for telemetry, and they were the densest thing on a
  newcomer's screen. `H` toggles it, and the three military styles (retro,
  surveillance, thermal) still bring the HUD with them, so choosing a tactical
  look is unchanged.
- HUD readouts stay legible over bright imagery. The scope mask had been
  darkening the corners those readouts sit in; without it, cyan text landed on
  sunlit rooftops. The text now carries a tight dark halo under its coloured
  glow, and each corner block sits on a soft radial scrim with no edge or
  border — shade under the text rather than a panel around it.
