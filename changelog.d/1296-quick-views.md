- **Quick views** sit above the Data Layers list: Traffic & cameras, Aircraft,
  Ships, Weather, Earth events. The list answers which feeds exist; these
  answer what to switch on to watch something, which previously meant knowing
  which two or three of thirty rows to combine. A view is exact rather than
  additive — it enables its own layers and disables every other panel layer —
  so the same button always produces the same globe. Hidden coordinator layers
  are never switched off by a view, since other layers depend on them, and a
  layer a view names but the catalog does not carry is simply skipped rather
  than enabled blindly.
