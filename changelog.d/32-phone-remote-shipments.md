- The phone remote for the TV is now part of the app (opt-in with
  `GEV_REMOTE=1`; see WINDSOR.md), with Windsor mode, and gains a Shipments
  tab: your containers, most urgent first, with where each box is, when it
  loaded, its port or rail ETA and its last free day. Tap one to show its ship
  on the TV, or type a container number to add it. The phone can't remove or
  edit anything (#32).
